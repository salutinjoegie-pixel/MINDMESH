In the prototype phase of ChurchSearch, we developed the visual and functional blueprint of the web-based system. Team Reflection

"Making a prototype was challenging for us and our handler because we struggled to clearly imagine what we wanted our system to look like and how it should function. At first, it was hard to turn our ideas into a visual design since we were not sure how everything should be arranged and presented. Through this experience, we learned how important planning and teamwork are in creating a system. Even though it was challenging, it helped us better understand how to design a more organized and user-friendly prototype."

— Princess and Diana

Jeopardy

Our Jeopardy activity was very fun and enjoyable. We really had a great time answering the questions as a group. What made it more exciting was the scoring system, because if you get 2 points, it can turn into 20 to 50 points, which made everyone more competitive and motivated to answer correctly.

Team Reflection

"During the activity, we worked together and shared our ideas to come up with the right answers. Because of our teamwork and effort, our group was able to get the highest total of correct answers. This made us feel proud and happy because our hard work paid off. Overall, we enjoyed this activity so much because it was not only fun but also helped us learn while competing. It improved our thinking skills, teamwork, and confidence in answering questions."

— The MindMesh Team

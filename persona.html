Persona Mapping

Persona Mapping is a strategic methodology used to create detailed profiles of our target users. By identifying their demographics, motivations, and technological pain points, we ensure that the ChurchSearch system is designed with empathy and precision, catering to the specific needs of church administrators and record-seekers alike.

Lay's Reflection

"Developing a persona allowed me to gain a clearer understanding of a user’s needs, behaviors, and challenges. By outlining details like their age, goals, daily life, and struggles, I was able to view situations from their perspective instead of my own. During the process, I realized the importance of empathy in truly understanding people. It made me reflect on how individuals experience things differently and how their choices are shaped by their circumstances. This activity taught me that personas are valuable in planning and decision-making because they help create solutions that focus on the user."

— Lay Mae (Hustler)

Brainstorming

This task required us to brainstorm three titles that we needed to present on January 29, 2026. Our group shared different ideas and explained what each concept was about. We discussed the strengths and weaknesses of every idea and helped each other improve them. Maa'm gave us a scenario where we had to present our ideas using an elevator pitch, imagining that we were talking to a boss inside an elevator with only two to three minutes to explain everything. This activity helped us improve our confidence, communication skills, and teamwork, and it also taught us how to express ideas clearly in a short amount of time.
